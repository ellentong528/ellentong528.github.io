I have always enjoyed working with children in my research. As an undergrad at UC Berkeley, I was a research assistant in the [Computation and Language Lab](https://colala.berkeley.edu/), and a lead research assistant and honors thesis student at the [Berkeley Early Learning Lab](https://babylab.berkeley.edu/). I wrote my honors thesis under the mentorship of Professor Fei Xu on young children's understanding of posterior probabilities ([thesis](/files/Tong-E-Thesis-Sp25.pdf); [poster](/files/tong-thesis-poster); you can also find details of my research below). I am currently a master's student at the Harvard Graduate School of Education, and have been a research assistant in the Snedeker Lab in the Department of Psychology and in the Child-Centered AI Lab at the Harvard Graduate School of Education.
