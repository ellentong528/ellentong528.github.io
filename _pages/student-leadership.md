Sometimes I don’t really like the word _leader_ or _leadership_, because, to some extent, either makes it easy to assume that others are just following. It doesn't work like that. Every day, I’m reminded of how deeply I’m loved by the people I’ve met and how inspired I am by the connections I’ve made. Only because of that have I felt comfortable enough to show love to others. Leadership wouldn’t be the same without love—and I wouldn’t change a thing about that.

In one year, five years, ten years, or for the rest of my life, I hope I’ll still be surrounded by people who make me feel loved and inspired by the new connections I’ll create. And I hope I’ll continue to show my love to them as openly and unconditionally as I always have. “_We don’t have a word for the opposite of loneliness, but if we did, I’d say that’s how I feel… right now. Here. With all of you. In love, impressed, humbled, scared. And we don’t have to lose that._”

In the next couple of weeks, I will be updating this page with my experiences as a student leader/servant. Please stay tuned for perhaps the most memorable moments that have shaped me into who I am today.
