Hi all! My name is Ellen Tong. I grew up in Beijing, China and have been a proud international student since 2017. I received my B.A. in Psychology with a minor in Education from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/) in May 2025, and my Ed.M. in Human Development and Education at the [Harvard Graduate School of Education](https://www.gse.harvard.edu/).

I am currently the Lab Manager in the [Brain and Cognitive Development Lab](https://publish.illinois.edu/danielchyde/) at the [University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign](illinois.edu). Our lab investigates the development of numerical cognition using EEG, fNIRS, and behavioral tasks.

To check out my **academic interests and experiences**, please refer to my [Research](/research) page.

If you’d like to learn more about what I did in my college community, please refer to my [Student Leadership](/student-leadership) page.
